What is Cambridge Exam Prep Kit?

An offline practice app for Cambridge English qualifications, covering Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking, and Use of English at levels A2 Key through C2 Proficiency — with diagnostics, guided practice, and full mock exams.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All content, audio, and practice material ships with the app and works fully offline.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-in and no account of any kind — just open the app and start practicing.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the Profile tab and use the reset option there. Since progress is stored only on your device, deleting and reinstalling the app also clears it completely.

Is my data private?

Yes — see the Privacy Policy for details. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.
