What is Cambridge Exam Prep Kit?

An offline practice app for Cambridge English qualifications, covering Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking, and Use of English at levels A2 Key through C2 Proficiency — with diagnostics, guided practice, and full mock exams.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All content, audio, and practice material ships with the app and works fully offline.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed to use the free sample. Sign in with Apple is only asked for right before starting a paid subscription, to confirm who's paying — it's never required just to open the app or try it out.

What's free, and what requires a subscription?

The placement diagnostic is always free, and every new learner gets five free practice questions to try the app. After that, a Premium subscription (monthly or yearly) unlocks unlimited practice questions, full timed mock exams, and progress tracking across all five papers.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open the Profile tab and tap Manage Subscription, or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple.

I subscribed but the app says I haven't — what do I do?

Open the Profile tab and tap Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID's purchase history and restores an active subscription without charging you again.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the Profile tab and use the reset option there. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the app also clears it. The one exception is the five-question free sample, which is remembered even through a reinstall so it stays a one-time offer.

Is my data private?

Yes — see the Privacy Policy for details. Your practice, answers, and recordings never leave your device.
