CAMBRIDGE EXAM PREP KIT

Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Short version: Cambridge Exam Prep Kit does not run its own servers and does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with anyone. Your practice progress always stays on your device. The only outside party involved at all is Apple, and only for signing in and processing a subscription — both described below.

No accounts of our own, no servers of our own

The app itself has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no server. It works fully offline, and there is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party SDK of any kind embedded in the app.

The one exception is Sign in with Apple, which the app offers only immediately before starting a subscription purchase, and only to confirm who's paying — nothing else in the app uses it, nothing syncs, and no profile is built from it. That sign-in, and any subscription purchase, is handled entirely by Apple's own systems (Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchase); the app never sees your Apple ID, name, email, or payment details, and has no server of its own for any of that to go to. The one piece of information the app keeps is the anonymous identifier Apple issues for that sign-in, stored only in your device's Keychain, used solely to remember that you're signed in.

What's stored, and where

Your practice progress, saved drafts, and any Speaking-practice recordings you make are stored only in the app's local database and Documents folder, on your device. None of this data is uploaded anywhere, shared with the developer, or shared with any third party.

Microphone use

The Speaking practice feature uses your device's microphone so you can record and review your own responses. Recordings are processed and stored entirely on-device and are never transmitted off the device.

Subscriptions

Premium access is offered as an auto-renewable subscription, billed and managed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. The app never handles or sees your payment information. You can review, change, or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or any similar tracking technology.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device deletes all of its locally stored data, including progress and recordings. There is no server-side copy to remove because none is ever created. One narrow exception: a small counter that only records how many free sample questions have been used is kept in the device Keychain so that number survives a reinstall — this exists solely to keep the free sample a one-time offer, carries no other information, and is separate from your Apple ID identifier described above.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from any user, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
