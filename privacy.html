CAMBRIDGE EXAM PREP KIT

Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Short version: Cambridge Exam Prep Kit does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, from anyone, ever. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

No accounts, no network calls

The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and makes no network requests. It works fully offline. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party SDK of any kind embedded in the app.

What's stored, and where

Your practice progress, saved drafts, and any Speaking-practice recordings you make are stored only in the app's local database and Documents folder, on your device. None of this data is uploaded anywhere, shared with the developer, or shared with any third party.

Microphone use

The Speaking practice feature uses your device's microphone so you can record and review your own responses. Recordings are processed and stored entirely on-device and are never transmitted off the device.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or any similar tracking technology.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device deletes all of its locally stored data, including progress and recordings. There is no server-side copy to remove because none is ever created.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from any user, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to chamowil@outlook.com.
